Your birth chart holds a precise map of how you love, what you need from a partner, and why certain patterns keep showing up in your relationships. The Love Blueprint reads that map, in your chart specifically.

Using Whole Sign houses and the Swiss Ephemeris, it looks at your Venus, your Moon, your Mars, your 7th and 8th houses, and the nodes, building a portrait of your love nature that goes far beyond Sun sign compatibility.

The result is a report specific enough to name the patterns you have always sensed, and grounded enough to actually help you do something with them.
